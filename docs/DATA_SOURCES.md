## 2. Ingested GTFS Entities

- **`agency.txt`**: Transit operator metadata (BMTC).
- **`routes.txt`**: 1,800+ bus routes across Bangalore metropolitan region.
- **`trips.txt`**: Scheduled directional transit trips.
- **`stops.txt`**: 9,800+ verified bus stops with geocoded coordinates (WGS84 lat/lon).
- **`stop_times.txt`**: Sequence order, arrival/departure schedules.
- **`fare_attributes.txt` & `fare_rules.txt`**: Distance-based fare stages and zonal rules.

---

## 3. Synthetic Simulation Datasets

Generated by Phase 4 and Phase 5 simulation engines with deterministic random seed (`42`):
- `data/synthetic/ticket_events.csv`: Individual boarding and payment records across 100 benchmark routes and 200 scheduled trips.
- `data/synthetic/trip_summaries.csv`: Nominal aggregated trip operational metrics.
- `data/synthetic/anomalous_trip_summaries.csv`: Trip metrics containing realistic injected revenue deficits.
- `data/synthetic/anomaly_ground_truth.csv`: Isolated oracle metadata strictly sequestered for model evaluation.
